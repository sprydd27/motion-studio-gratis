# Third-party footage rights gate

## Establish the rights basis before you download

- Read the channel rules file first when one exists and use its rights list as the only list that counts.

- Establish one basis before you download, transcribe, or cut any footage you did not create.

- Accept a clipping campaign only when it grants rights for this footage and this use, and record the campaign name and link.

- Accept creator permission only when the creator allows clipping of this footage, and record the permission and its source link.

- Accept owner gameplay only when the footage shows the owner playing, and record how you verified that.

- Accept an official platform clip only when it comes from the platform official clip feature plus streamer credit on screen and in the description plus a transformative compilation, and record the clip link.

- Treat a basis approved for one platform as limited to that platform, so a Twitch clip basis does not clear a full YouTube video re-cut.

- Treat a cutting playbook as a cutting method only, never as rights clearance.

- Stop and ask the owner when no basis fits, and do not download or cut while you wait.

## Record the basis and get each clip approved

- Write the basis, the source link, the streamer handle, and the credit plan into the channel rules file before you cut.

- Keep the source link and streamer handle on screen and in the description for every third-party clip.

- Skip story spoilers, cutscenes, endings, leaks, exploit methods, and anything the rights holder asked to take down.

- Send each cut through the Gauntlet before you publish it, and publish only after that gate says YES and the owner approves that specific clip.

- Treat a broad delegation to manage the channel as permission to arrange work, never as clearance of footage rights.

- Treat one clip approval as approval for that clip only, never as approval for other clips from the same source.

## Treat held footage as held

- Leave a clip held when it has no recorded basis, no Gauntlet YES, or no owner approval for that clip.

- Describe held clips as held, never as ready to schedule later, and do not schedule or post them until all three conditions are met.

- Ask the owner to seek clipping permission, join a campaign, or hold the clips unposted indefinitely when the decision is still open.

- Do not download or cut further third-party long-form videos while the held clips have no basis.

## Alternatives for better results

- Build the episode from footage the owner recorded themselves when no third-party basis is available.

- Use original commentary, labels, and structure to make a compilation transformative instead of a straight repost.

- Ask the owner early whether to seek permission or join a campaign, so the choice is made before any cutting starts.
